## Public API and schema

`evaluateTrafficSourceHealth({evaluatedAt,sources})` is synchronous, pure, deterministic. `evaluatedAt` is caller-supplied UTC milliseconds (nonnegative safe integer). `sources` is an array of 0–1000 records in caller order. Each record must have a unique nonblank `sourceId`, positive safe-integer `expectedIntervalMs` and `maxAgeMs`, `lastSuccessAt` and `lastAttemptAt` (nonnegative safe integer UTC milliseconds or explicit null), nonnegative safe-integer `consecutiveFailures`, `permissionState` one of `declared-allowed`, `unknown`, `denied`, and `licenseRef` nonblank string or null. No type coercion, defaults, ambient clock or network.

A missing success means `no-data`. With success, age is `evaluatedAt - lastSuccessAt` in **milliseconds**; `age >= maxAgeMs` is stale, exactly at the boundary. A fresh age `>= expectedIntervalMs` produces `LATE_UPDATE`, not stale. Last attempt earlier than last success, future timestamps, invalid configuration, or duplicate source IDs produce per-source `invalid`. Duplicate IDs invalidate **all** occurrences. A recent failed attempt yields `RECENT_FAILURES` warning while retaining `fresh` transport when the previous successful data is still within maxAge.

Each result source includes `index,sourceId,transportState,permissionState,usableForEvaluation,degraded,errors,warnings` (and `ageMs` for structurally valid records). `usableForEvaluation` requires `fresh` + `declared-allowed` + nonempty `licenseRef`. Permission is **only caller declaration**, not a legal license check. No coverage, truth, passability, vehicle-applicability or truck-safety assertion.

Aggregate: `usable` when all sources are usable and no warning; `degraded` if at least one usable but any warnings/invalid/stale/unusable; `unavailable` if none usable, including an empty list; `invalid-input` for invalid envelope (`evaluatedAt` or `sources` shape/length). Aggregate includes reason, evaluatedAt, per-source diagnostics and total/usable/invalid counts. Invalid records do not hide other records.

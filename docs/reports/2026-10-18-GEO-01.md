## Behavior

`indexRoute` validates coordinates and creates cumulative spherical distances while retaining input segment indexes. Consecutive duplicate points are harmless; routes with no positive length are rejected. `pointAtOffset` interpolates along a great-circle segment and rejects non-finite or out-of-range offsets.

`projectToRoute` projects onto each eligible great-circle segment, not just vertices. It clips segments to optional offset bounds, reports the nearest candidate and lateral distance, and returns `off-route` when the corridor is exceeded. Distinct, similarly close route offsets yield `ambiguous` with candidates and no selected position. Adjacent-segment candidates within 5 m along the route are treated as the same local position; shared-vertex duplicates are also collapsed. The supplied `routeRevision` is returned unchanged; no cross-revision result cache exists.

## Accuracy and limits

Distances use a spherical Earth radius of 6,371,000 m (the convention in `engine/route.js` at the requested base). Segments are interpreted as minor great-circle arcs. This is a practical spherical approximation, not an ellipsoidal geodesic; distance differences from WGS-84 can be on the order of 0.5%, with additional approximation when route geometry is sparse or curved between points. The module rejects coordinates at or beyond 85° latitude, arcs entering that polar region, longitude jumps of 180° or more (antimeridian or ambiguous span), and near-antipodal segments.

Projection only identifies a geometric location relative to the supplied line. It does not check road accessibility, travel direction, traffic, or truck suitability.
